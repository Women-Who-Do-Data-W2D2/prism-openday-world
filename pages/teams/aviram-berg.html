Do Rules and Concepts Hold Across Languages?

Mentor: Aviram Berg

Lecturer on AI Safety; Founder, CCI

About this project

AI safety and compliance rules are almost exclusively written in English, but the models being deployed operate across many languages. This project investigates: when a rule is embedded into a model's internal representation space in one language, does it hold in another? And when it degrades, how and why?
